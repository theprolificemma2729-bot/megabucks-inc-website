MEGABUCKS INC. WEBSITE

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the megabucks-inc-website.zip file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Open index.html in a browser.
4. Click the division cards to visit the separate division pages.

PROJECT STRUCTURE
index.html                 Main homepage
finance.html               Finance division page
travel.html                Travel & Consultancy division page
technology.html            Technology division page
real-estate.html           Real Estate division page
investments.html           Investments division page
trading.html               Trading & Supplies division page

css/finance.css             Finance page styling

css/technology.css          Technology page styling
css/real-estate.css         Real Estate page styling
css/investments.css         Investments page styling

CUSTOMISE
- Replace info@megabucks.example with your official email address.
- Add your telephone number, physical address and social media links.
- Update each division page with confirmed services and contact details.
- The website is a starter template. It does not process payments or collect enquiries yet.
- Finance and other regulated services should only be advertised/offered once the relevant licences and approvals are in place.

The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so an internet connection is needed for those fonts. The site layout and content still work without them.
